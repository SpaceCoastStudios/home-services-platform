This is the embeddable booking form your customers will see. It collects their contact info, service request, and — critically — their explicit SMS consent before submission.

How the widget works

The booking widget is a lightweight iframe you paste into any website with a single line of code. It loads your business's service list, branding, and consent language automatically — no developer needed after the initial setup.

  • 📋 Collects name, phone, email, service type, and a message from the customer
  • ✅ Requires explicit SMS opt-in checkbox before the form can be submitted
  • 🤖 Submission triggers an instant AI response via SMS to the customer's phone
  • 📅 AI checks your availability and books the appointment directly in the conversation
  • 🎨 Styled to match your brand color — no coding required
  • 📱 Fully responsive — works on mobile, tablet, and desktop

SMS Opt-In Compliance

The checkbox below the form is a required field — customers cannot submit without checking it. This provides documented, TCPA-compliant opt-in consent for all automated SMS messages sent through your platform.
