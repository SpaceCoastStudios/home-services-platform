Recurring Weekly Service, On Autopilot

Weekly service is the heart of a pool business, so the platform treats it as first-class. What that means in practice

Visits generate themselves. Each week's appointments are created automatically from the schedule and show up on the dashboard and the tech's daily list.

Customers stay in the loop. Reminders and on-the-way texts fire for recurring visits the same as one-time jobs, so "did the pool guy come today?" calls disappear.

Skips and changes are one click. Pause a vacation week, change the day, or swap the tech without touching the rest of the schedule.

Reviews compound. Completed visits can trigger Google review requests automatically, turning your steadiest customers into your loudest ones.
