What you get

A complete platform, built and managed for you.

We handle everything — design, setup, and ongoing support. You get a professional booking system without hiring a developer.

🤖

AI Contact Responder

Every inquiry from your website gets a personalized, professional reply within seconds — day or night. The AI knows your services and can suggest real appointment times, 24/7.

📱

SMS Booking Agent

Customers text your business number and the AI handles it from start to finish — answers questions, checks availability, and books the appointment. No app, no login, just a text.

📅

Self-Scheduling Booking Widget

Customers see your real availability and pick their own time slot — right on your website. The system checks your calendar, blocks booked times, auto-assigns the right tech, and confirms the booking instantly. No phone calls, no back-and-forth.

🚨

Emergency Dispatch

When a customer texts about an emergency, the AI asks the right questions, discloses any after-hours fee, and — once the customer confirms — texts your on-call technician immediately.

📬

Automated Notifications

Confirmations, 24-hour reminders, and "your tech is on the way" alerts — all sent automatically by SMS and email. Fewer no-shows, less manual follow-up, happier customers.

⭐

Automated Review Requests

The moment a job is marked complete, the system sends your customer a review request with a direct link to your Google listing. More reviews, less asking.

🚗

On The Way Notifications

An hour before a job, your tech gets an SMS prompt. When they reply YES, the customer is notified their tech is on the way — and a second YES marks the job done and fires the review request.

🎨

Fully Branded & Embeddable

Your logo, colors, and AI persona throughout. Choose a contact form widget to capture leads (Starter) or a full self-scheduling booking widget with live availability (Professional) — both embed into any existing website with a single line of code.

📊

Business Dashboard

One clean view of your appointments, customers, technicians, and leads. Manage your on-call schedule, review AI responses before they send, and see everything that needs your attention.

See It In Action

Try the booking widget — right here

This is the actual widget your customers will use. Fill it out to see how the AI booking flow works — and notice the SMS consent checkbox required by carriers before any text messages can be sent.

What happens when a customer fills this out?

The moment they submit, our AI sends a confirmation SMS, adds them to your schedule, and notifies your tech team — all automatically, 24/7.

  • 📅 Instant booking confirmation via SMS & email
  • 🤖 AI responds to questions around the clock
  • 🔔 Automated reminders cut no-shows by up to 40%
  • ⭐ Post-job review request sent automatically
  • 🔧 On-the-way technician notifications keep customers informed

📋 TCPA / SMS Compliance

Carriers require explicit written consent before any business can send SMS messages. The checkbox on this form is that consent — it's required by federal law (TCPA) and carrier policy (A2P 10DLC). Customers cannot submit without checking it.
