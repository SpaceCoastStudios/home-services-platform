Common questions

Everything owners ask us before they sign up.

How fast can we go live?
Your platform is typically live and taking bookings within one week: contact form, booking widget, email notifications, and dashboard. Text messaging requires a one-time carrier registration (called A2P 10DLC) that takes 2 to 4 weeks to approve. We submit it on day one, so everything else runs while we wait, and SMS features switch on automatically once carriers approve.
Do I need a new website?
No. Both widgets embed into your existing website with a single line of code, and they carry your logo and colors. If you don't have a website yet, every widget also works as a standalone shareable link you can put on your Google listing, social pages, or truck decals.
What if the AI says something wrong?
You're in control. You can run the AI in auto-send mode or draft mode, where your staff reviews and approves every AI reply before it goes out. Anything unusual, like a real emergency or a question the AI can't answer confidently, is escalated to a human on your team immediately by text and email.
Who does all the setup?
We do. Services, technicians, booking rules, business hours, notification templates, AI persona, and branding are all configured for you, and we walk your team through the dashboard before you go live. That's what the setup fee covers, and the monthly retainer keeps us maintaining and supporting it after launch.
How is setup priced?
Setup starts at $499 and depends on how much we build for you: the size of your team, how many services you offer, whether you need on-call and emergency dispatch, and whether we import your existing customers or recurring routes. We give you the exact amount on your demo call, before you commit to anything.
Am I locked into a long contract?
Our agreement is straightforward, and we walk you through every term before you sign. No surprise fees, and your monthly rate can never change without at least 60 days written notice.
What trades do you work with?
HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, roofing, pest control, pool service, cleaning, and more. If your business takes appointments, we can build your platform around it. Reach out and tell us what you do.
